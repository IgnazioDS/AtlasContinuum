## World of Warcraft assets

The OpenMap license explicitly covers code, not Blizzard map art. The addon's
Retail texture directory contains masks cut from World of Warcraft map art and
textures generated from noise. Their upstream provenance is preserved in
`OpenMap/Media/Textures/Retail/NOTICE.md`.

Before public redistribution, confirm that the packaging and distribution of
these derived assets complies with Blizzard's current UI Add-On Development
Policy and platform terms. This notice is provenance documentation, not a
grant of rights to Blizzard assets or trademarks.
